Velty

A pause before you scroll

Velty adds one calm breath before Instagram, TikTok and other feeds open. Then you choose: open for a few minutes, or change your mind.

Free. No ads, no subscriptions.

Nothing leaves your phone. No account, no server — your data stays on your device.

Gentle. No shame, no guilt counters — just “changed my mind” and “opened”.

Velty is in private beta on iPhone.
